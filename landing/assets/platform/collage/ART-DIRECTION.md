Make the metaphor clear at a 350px card width, with only 2–3 major visual elements, strong silhouettes. All important subjects must fit above y=700 of the 1024px image; bottom must be blank paper. Avoid cutting off heads or meaningful props.

## Responsive fit

Sources are 1536 × 1024; the card media well is 2:1 and preserves the full image width, trimming only the reserved blank paper at the bottom. Copy is a separate panel with dark text, so it never covers subjects. A short paper-colored fade softens the image-to-copy boundary. The former zoom animation is removed to keep these deliberately composed crops stable. Every audience uses six distinct assets. Other page photography is unchanged.
